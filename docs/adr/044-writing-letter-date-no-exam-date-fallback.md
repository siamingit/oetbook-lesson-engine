# 044 — Writing: the letter's date is the date the paper gives; the exam-date fallback is dropped

Date: 2026-10-27
Status: **Accepted**: the owner's correction of 2026-10-27, given through the chat in the brief "Build
Writing 3, 'Letter structure'". It **amends [ADR 040](040-writing-course-content-decisions.md) decision 3**:
the rule "if the notes give no date, the exam date" is removed from the owner's unofficial rules. A new
record, not an edit of ADR 040, because AGENTS.md §10 and ADR 040's own consequences say a reversal of a
decision is a new ADR. How it is recorded was decided by the agent under that brief (ADR 005).

## Context

- In Session 3 (writing-03-letter-structure, 09:28–10:31) the teacher says that if the case notes give no
  date, you write the date of your exam. ADR 040 decision 3 kept it as his advice, "said briefly"
  (docs/writing-facts.md; ANALYSIS.md §5.7 R3).
- The owner now says the date is always given. Each of our six Writing papers (oetacademy-exercises
  5320d19) gives it at the top, above the case notes: "Assume that today's date is …".
- OET's own article says the same. https://oet.com/post/writing-the-date-in-the-right-place-on-your-letter,
  read by the agent on 2026-10-27: "The date for your letter will usually be given at the start of the
  writing test, like this: Assume that today's date is ...."; "Use that date."; "The position of the date
  can be either above the recipient's address or below the address."; "In formal letters, it is normal for
  the date at the start to be written in full form e.g. 2 October 2021 rather than 2/10/21."

## Options considered

- Keep the fallback as the teacher's advice (ADR 040 decision 3): rejected by the owner.
- Drop it and teach only the date the paper gives (chosen, the owner's decision).

## Decision (owner, 2026-10-27)

1. **The exam-date fallback is not taught, in any form**: not as the teacher's advice, not as a
   "what if". It leaves the list of the owner's rules in ADR 040 decision 3 and docs/writing-facts.md.
2. **What the lessons teach instead**: the letter's date is the date the paper gives at the top ("Assume
   that today's date is …"). It is never the admission date, the discharge date or any other date in the
   notes (those go in the body, if the reader needs them). It goes above or below the reader's address,
   written in full ("16 October 2026"). docs/writing-facts.md fact 44.

## Consequences

- docs/writing-facts.md: the rule's row under "The owner's other teaching rules" and its row under "What
  the source sessions say, checked" now say it is dropped, and fact 44 (the date, from OET's article) is
  added, VERIFIED.
- `C:\OET\lessons\_writing-analysis\ANALYSIS.md` §5.7 R3 (outside the repository) says "dropped by the
  owner, 2026-10-27 (ADR 044)".
- No lesson taught the fallback: Writing 1 and 2 never mention it (checked: no utterance or board of
  either says "exam" with "date"). Writing 3 is the first lesson to teach the date and teaches decision 2.
- Found while recording this, not decided here: OET's same article names "Dear Doctor" as an example
  salutation ("plus a salutation (e.g. Dear Doctor) and an Re: line"); ADR 040 decision 7 says "Dear
  Doctor" is not taught. Decision 7 stands; this is for the owner.
- To reverse: a new ADR. The decision is the owner's.
