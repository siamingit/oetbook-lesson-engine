# Bundle format 1.22: style C and its motion, for the site

Written 2026-10-26 for oetacademy-web, sent as its issue #173 (label `from-lesson-engine`). It
is the engine's changelog entry for bundle format 1.22, §9b of `docs/bundle-format-changelog.md`,
word for word, with what the site must do. Engine record: docs/adr/042-style-c-and-motion.md.
Amended 2026-10-26 after the owner's review of Writing 2 (ADR 042, amendment (2)): the changes from the
first text are listed at the top of §9b, and the comment on issue #173 that sends this text. Amended
again 2026-10-27, when the 22 published lessons were restyled (ADR 043): listed at the top of §9b.

**Before anything else:** the site's contracts `contracts-v1.10.0`, which the engine pins, refuse a
1.22 bundle (`format_version` and the manifest's `lesson.bundle_format_version` allow `"1.8"` to
`"1.21"`), and the site's player plays 1.8 to 1.21 only (`PLAYABLE_BUNDLE_FORMATS`). The engine will
not package or publish any 1.22 lesson until the site releases contracts that accept 1.22 and the
engine pins them. The first 1.22 lesson is Writing 2, `writing-02-assessment-criteria` (built, at its
final gate, not published).

## 9b. Format 1.22 — style C, "soft notebook", and its motion

2026-10-26 (engine ADR 042), released as the tag `bundle-format-v1.22` of the engine's repository. The
owner chose a new look for the lessons, style C, for Writing 2 and every lesson built after it, with
motion effects; the 22 published lessons keep their look and their format. A lesson is 1.22 only when
it is in style C.

**Amended 2026-10-26, after the owner's review of Writing 2** (engine ADR 042, amendment (2)), before
any site had built 1.22, so the format keeps its number. What changed from the first text:

- **The marker** (rule 53): a light amber `#ffe19c` behind the lower 38% of the line (was `#ffd27a`,
  42%), and its multiply must reach the words: nothing between the band and the frame may make a group
  of its own (a positioned element with a z-index, an opacity under 1, a transform, a filter). The first
  text's band sat in a marks layer with a z-index, so it was laid flat over the letters and the owner saw
  the words covered.
- **The strike** (rule 53): `#b42318` (was `#b83b28`).
- **The ground**: white, the frame, the header, the board and the controls (was lilac `#f3f0fb`). The
  site has no ground to set: drop "let the frame take the ground" from what the site must do.
- **The lift and the spotlight** (rules 11 and 54): no outline and no lilac ring; a soft neutral shadow
  and a very small scale, inside the table's or the board's edges; a row lifts as one, its cells keep
  their tints. All in `blocks.css`: nothing for the player.
- **The colours and the layouts**: the handouts' tones by meaning, each of the six OET Writing criteria
  in its own colour, tables with a solid coloured header row, and board layouts (tiles, scorecards,
  cards, steps, a flow, bins, a fix, a stack, case notes, changes, a checklist, a letter, two options
  side by side). All in the block `html` (classes and attributes) and `blocks.css`: nothing for the
  player.
- No field is added or removed.

**Amended 2026-10-27, when the 22 published lessons were restyled** (engine ADR 043), still before any
site had built 1.22, so the format keeps its number:

- **A contents item's sections** (rule 53): an item that lists two or more sections of its category has
  `class="cs parts"` and each section in a `<span class="cp">`; no word changes, the " · " between them
  stays in the text and is drawn at no size. A mark on a section (the narration names them one by one)
  lies inside its `cp`.
- **Where the engine writes a zoom**: only on a Writing lesson's documents (a letter, case notes). The
  rule is unchanged; a lesson of another type simply has no zoom entries.
- **The stylesheet** (nothing for the player): a document's paragraphs (`.tbl.core.doc`: a Reading text,
  a Listening transcript) on paper with no rule between them, its active paragraph on paper too (a
  keyword pair's `match1`–`match3` band, multiplied with what is under it, keeps its colour); a header
  cell in a tense family's colour (`th.fam-past`, `fam-now`, ...) keeps it; typed text in a table with no
  layout is set without kerning or ligatures, so the typed and untyped halves of a cell add up to its
  line.
- **The 22 lessons published before Writing 2 are now 1.22** (style C, phase 1: no board layouts yet).
  They are packaged, as new package versions, once the site accepts 1.22.
- No field is added or removed.

### What is in `blocks.css` (no new data, plays on today's player)

A style C lesson's `blocks.css` is the 1.21 stylesheet followed by style C's rules. They key only on
the classes and attributes a player already sets or the block `html` already carries:

- the look: a white frame, header band, board and controls; notes on a soft neutral tint, the board's
  own sentences on paper, no borders; word cards, "Meaning" and "Word" in the handouts' gloss amber;
  right olive `#556b00`, wrong red `#b42318`; a table with a solid header row in its section's colour
  and white words in sentence case, tinted body rows; type `var(--font-plex, "IBM Plex Sans")`, 400 to
  700 (the site's `--font-plex` is IBM Plex Sans);
- the column labels' meaning: a table's `th` whose words say wrong or right, and that column's `td`
  cells on a core table, carry `data-tone="wrong"` or `data-tone="right"` in the block `html` (red
  `#b42318` with a cross, olive `#556b00` with a tick);
- **the six OET Writing criteria**: every block of a section named after a criterion carries
  `data-crit` (`purpose`, `content`, `cc`, `gs`, `ol`, `lang`) in its `html`, and the stylesheet
  colours it; a term box "The criterion" there is drawn as the criterion's card (`crit`, `data-mono`:
  its monogram in a circle);
- **the board layouts** (engine docs/02-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §7j): a block's `html` may carry `lay
  lay-<layout>` and the attributes and empty elements its layout reads (`data-label`, `data-bin`,
  `data-mono`, `data-yes`, `data-l`, `n2`/`n4`, `num`, empty `<i>` and `<span aria-hidden="true">`);
  only a table's rows and cells are laid out, its hooks (`data-row`, `data-col`, `data-typed`) stay, no
  word is added, and the renderer draws the `html` as given;
- **a contents item's sections** (2026-10-27): `cs parts` and a `<span class="cp">` per section, set one
  under the other under the category's heading;
- **soft appear**: `.blk.on` that is not a fixed block (`[data-layer]` / `[data-oa-layer]` not
  `fixed`) runs a 0.3 s animation, opacity 0 and 1.2% of the frame lower to its place
  (`animation-fill-mode: backwards`);
- **the spotlight in style C** (rule 11): rows not in focus at opacity 0.5 (not 0.3); the row in focus
  (`.tbl.core.spot tbody tr.focus`) lifted as one row, inside the table's edges: a soft neutral shadow
  (`filter: drop-shadow(0 .6cqh 1.2cqh rgba(20,32,31,.14))` on a table row, `box-shadow: 0 .8cqh 2.8cqh
  rgba(20,32,31,.12)` on a tile or a card) and `transform: scale(1.004)` (1.015 for a tile or a card),
  over 0.25 s; its cells keep their tints; no ring, no outline;
- **the lift's look** (rule 54): `.blk.fx-lift` scaled 1.01 (a contents item 1.02), the shadow
  `0 .8cqh 2.8cqh rgba(20,32,31,.12)`, `z-index: 2`, no outline;
- `.blk` takes `transition: opacity .25s ease, transform .25s cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), box-shadow .25s
  ease !important`, so it wins over the player's unlayered `.blk { transition: opacity .25s }`;
- `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`: no soft appear, no scale, transitions of opacity only.

Everything a lift or the spotlight scales uses `scale(calc(1 + k * var(--sc-motion, 1)))`; the
engine's measuring checks set `--sc-motion: 0` to measure the board at rest. A player need not set it.

### Added: `lesson.style`

| Field | Type | Values | Meaning | When absent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lesson.style` | string, optional | `"c"` (the only value) | The lesson is drawn in style C: rules 53 to 55 | the lesson's look is its `blocks.css` alone, as before |

### Added: `boards[].states[].effects`

On every state of a 1.22 lesson (an empty list when the state has none), in time order. Absent in every
other lesson.

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `type` | `"lift"` or `"zoom"` | rule 54 or rule 55 |
| `block` | block id | a block of the board: the block lifted; for a zoom, the board's `table` |
| `time`, `until` | seconds | from when to when, inside the state (`start` ≤ `time` < `until` ≤ `until` of the state) |
| `row` | integer | a zoom: the table's body row (from 0) |
| `col` | integer or null | a zoom: the cell's column (from 0), or null for the whole row |
| `text` | string or null | a zoom: the words to zoom onto, as they occur in that cell, or null for the whole cell |

```json
{"id": "page-2_t2.s1", "working": ["page-2_k12", "page-2_k03", "page-2_k04", "…"],
 "effects": [{"type": "lift", "block": "page-2_k03", "time": 41.204, "until": 44.910},
             {"type": "lift", "block": "page-2_k04", "time": 44.910, "until": 48.022}],
 "…": "…"}
{"id": "page-9_t1.s4", "row": 1,
 "effects": [{"type": "zoom", "block": "page-9_k01", "row": 1, "col": 1, "text": "12 March 2026",
              "time": 610.402, "until": 614.118}],
 "…": "…"}
```

### Rule 53, style C

In a lesson whose `lesson.style` is `"c"`, the look is the lesson's `blocks.css`, keyed on the classes
rules 4, 11 and 18 already set and on `fx-lift` (rule 54). The renderer draws what it draws itself in
style C's looks and motion:

- a `highlight` mark is a marker band, `#ffe19c`, behind the lower 38% of each line box of its phrase
  (`linear-gradient(transparent 62%, #ffe19c 62%)`, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`), and the multiply must
  reach the words: no element between the band and the frame may make a group of its own (a positioned
  element with a z-index, an opacity under 1, a transform, a filter), or the band is laid flat over the
  letters. So a letter stays as dark as it is and only its ground takes the amber, on any card and in a
  lifted row. (The engine's reference player gives its marks layer no z-index in style C and each mark
  box its own; a layer with a z-index, as `.oa-marks` has today, isolates the band.) A `strike` is
  `#b42318`; the other marks as before;
- a `highlight` is drawn in from left to right over 0.3 s from its `time`, a `strike` over 0.25 s,
  line after line: the share drawn is `(t - time) / duration` of the phrase's total width (the sum of
  its line fragments), computed from the lesson's clock, so a seek shows the mark whole. (The site
  rebuilds its mark boxes every frame, so a CSS animation on them would restart; the width must come
  from the clock, as the engine's reference player does.)
- from an erase's `time`, the working blocks fade out and shrink to 96% over 0.25 s, then go; a block a
  clear names does the same, and its height and the gap after it close to nothing over those 0.25 s,
  so the blocks after it rise into its room instead of jumping (reference: the block keeps its place
  until `time + 0.25`, with `max-height` from its own height to 0, padding to 0 and
  `margin-bottom: -<the board's row gap>`);
- the camera glides over 0.35 s, eased (rules 13 and 55).

Under `prefers-reduced-motion` none of it moves: marks appear whole, blocks go at once. At rest the
board is exactly as without the motion, and no word moves inside its block (rule 19).

### Rule 54, a lift

While a state is current and not erased, from a `lift` entry's `time` to its `until`, the element of
`block` carries the class `fx-lift`; the lesson's `blocks.css` draws it. The renderer only sets and
clears the class (a seek sets it at once). Never on a fixed block's position: the lift is a transform
about its centre.

### Rule 55, a zoom

While a state is current and not erased, and not under `prefers-reduced-motion`, from a `zoom` entry's
`time` to its `until`, the view (the board's camera, as rule 13's) zooms onto the words `text` as they
occur in the cell (`row`, `col`) of the board's table `block` (the whole cell where `text` is null or
not found; the whole row where `col` is null), with every side note shown (rule 12):

- scale: the one that sets that cell's text at 3.2% of the frame's height on screen, never above 2.2,
  and no larger than keeps those words and the notes within 92% of the board's width and 80% of its
  height; below 1.03, no zoom;
- position: centred on them as far as the board's edges allow; the view never shows past the board's
  left, right, top or bottom (`translate(tx, ty) scale(s)` with transform origin at the board's top
  left, `tx` clamped to `[W - W*s, 0]` and `ty` to `[H - H*s, 0]`);
- it takes the place of rule 13 for that time, and applies on a board with `fit` `"whole"` too (rule
  52 holds outside it);
- the view glides in, from one entry to the next, and back out over 0.35 s.

Reference: `zoomView` in the engine's `spike/scripts/board_player_template.html`.

### Where the engine uses the effects

Lifts: each contents item while it is named; each of two or more fixed sentences or cards on a board
while it is read. Zooms: a document's line (a letter, case notes) set at 2.8% of the frame or less, or
on a board shown whole, from a mark the narration draws on it until the narration moves on to a note.
The table row in focus is lifted by `blocks.css` on the spotlight, with no data.

### Can a site that knows only 1.21 read 1.22?

- **The JSON is additive**: one optional lesson field and one optional state field.
- **The contract refuses it**: `format_version` enumerations, as for 1.21.
- **Played by a 1.21 player** (if the version check were relaxed): the look, the soft appear and the
  spotlight's lift and fade show (they are `blocks.css`); no block is lifted, nothing zooms, the marks
  keep their 1.21 looks and appear whole, and notes go at once. The lesson is readable but not as the
  owner chose it.

### Amended 2026-10-27 (2): a table spotlit by cell (rule 11)

Engine ADR 043, amendment of 2026-10-27, decision 17. Still before any site has built 1.22, so the format
keeps its number. **The site's player must change for it** (below); nothing in the contracts' shape beyond
one optional field.

**Why.** On a matching table, the left column lists words and the right column their meanings in a
shuffled order, so a row does not pair its two cells; the narration takes each word and draws an arrow
from it to its meaning in another row. Rule 11 dims every row but the one in focus, so it lit the word's
own row, the wrong meaning beside the word included, and left the right meaning, where the arrow points,
dimmed: a teaching error (the owner's report, Reading Part C (2), board `pages-13-14-15_t1` at 6:01; it
is in the published 1.21 package too). 39 boards of six lessons have such tables (Reading 3, 4 and 5,
Listening 3, 4 and 5: the word tables of their practice questions).

**Added: `boards[].focus[].cells`** (1.22, optional).

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `cells` | array of `[row, col]` (integers, from 0, the table's body rows and columns) | the cells lit while this entry is in force; present only on a board whose table does not pair its cells by row, and only on an entry with a `row` |

```json
"focus": [{"time": 341.198, "row": null, "col": null},
          {"time": 358.178, "row": 0, "col": null, "cells": [[0, 0]]},
          {"time": 360.128, "row": 0, "col": 0, "cells": [[0, 0], [5, 1]]},
          {"time": 362.792, "row": 5, "col": 1, "cells": [[0, 0], [5, 1]]},
          {"time": 390.638, "row": null, "col": null}]
```

The engine writes it from the lesson's own data: a board whose narration draws an arrow from a cell of
its table to a cell of another row. Each entry lists the cell the state's arrow starts from (the word
being taught), every cell its arrows have reached by then, and the cell being read or marked; an entry is
added at each arrow's time, so the meaning lights as the arrow is drawn, never before. `row` and `col`
are as before (the highlighted cell, rule 11).

**Rule 11, amended.** The spotlight is the latest `focus` entry whose `time` ≤ `t`. With `row` null
nothing is dimmed. Otherwise, if the entry has **no** `cells`, as before: rows other than `row` are
dimmed and the cell (`row`, `col`) is highlighted. If it **has** `cells`: **no row is in focus**; every
cell of the table body is dimmed except the cells listed, which are at full strength and highlighted like
the cell being taught; the cell (`row`, `col`) is highlighted as before. A cell beside a lit cell in its
row is dimmed like any other.

**What the site's player must do** (with the classes the engine's `blocks.css` keys on, so the lesson's
stylesheet draws it; a player that draws the spotlight itself does the same with its own look):

1. When the entry in force has `cells`, set on the table block the classes `spot` and `cellspot`; do
   **not** set `focus` on any `tr` (and so no row lift); set `active` on the cell (`row`, `col`) as
   before; set `lit` on each cell listed (`tbody tr[data-row=r] td[data-col=c]`), and remove `lit`
   from every other cell.
2. When the entry has no `cells`, remove `cellspot` and every `lit`, and set `focus` and `active` as
   before (rule 11 unchanged).
3. Seek: the same, from the entry in force at the new time (no replay).
4. The lesson's `blocks.css` (only in a lesson that has such a table) holds:
   `.tbl.core.spot.cellspot tbody tr td{opacity:.5}` and
   `.tbl.core.spot.cellspot tbody tr td.lit{opacity:1;background:var(--sc-active,#fff0c4)}`.
5. Auto zoom (rule 13) and every other rule: unchanged; the zoom goes to the cell (`row`, `col`).
6. Contracts: `boards[].focus[].cells` optional, `{"type": "array", "items": {"type": "array",
   "prefixItems": [{"type": "integer", "minimum": 0}, {"type": "integer", "minimum": 0}],
   "minItems": 2, "maxItems": 2}, "minItems": 1}`, only in 1.22 or later. Suggested rules: present only
   with a non-null `row`; every cell within the board's table.
7. Field index (§13): `1.22 bundle.boards[].focus[].cells`, `cells[]`, `cells[][]`.

A 1.21 player, or one that ignores `cells`, dims by row as before: the lesson plays, with the old error.

### What the site must do

1. **Contracts** (a new release): add `"1.22"` to `format_version` in `lesson-bundle.schema.json` and
   `lesson-text.schema.json` and to the manifest's `lesson.bundle_format_version`; `lesson.style`
   optional `{"enum": ["c"]}`; a state's `effects` optional, an array of `{type, block, time, until,
   row?, col?, text?}` with `type` `{"enum": ["lift", "zoom"]}`. Suggested rules for 1.22 or later: a
   lesson with `style` has `effects` on every state and the other way round; `effects` in time order;
   `block` on its board; a zoom's `block` the board's `table` and `row` one of its rows; `time` <
   `until`, both inside the state.
2. **Serving** (`packages/lesson-player/src/section/cut.ts`): pass `lesson.style` and each state's
   `effects` to the browser (the cut copies fields by name).
3. **Player**: accept 1.22 (`PLAYABLE_BUNDLE_FORMATS`); rules 53, 54 and 55, the marker band
   multiplied with the words under it (rule 53). The frame stays white, as the site's is: there is no
   ground to set.
4. **Reduced motion**: the site's global rule already cuts transitions to 0.01 ms; the player must also
   skip the marks' draw-in, the notes' going and the zoom in JavaScript.
5. **Tell the engine** the contracts release's tag and its zip's SHA-256: the engine pins it (a new
   amendment of its ADR 039), and only then packages and publishes Writing 2.
6. **Field index** (§13): `1.22 bundle.lesson.style`, `= "c"`, and the `effects` paths and values.
