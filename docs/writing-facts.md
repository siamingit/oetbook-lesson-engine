from it, before any Writing lesson is built. Facts 30–43 and the Sessions 4–6 rows were added

  fact until the owner rules on it.

## The owner's rule on word count

**OWNER'S RULE** (the owner, in the brief of 2026-10-23; his teaching rule, not an OET rule):

> Only the body of the letter is counted. 180–200 words is a range, not a hard limit, and ten or
> twelve words fewer or more do no harm. What matters is that all necessary information is
> included, unnecessary information is left out, and less important points are stated briefly.

It agrees with facts 14 to 16. A lesson says it in the first person, as the teacher's advice
(ADR 033 decision 1), next to the official guide.

correct version and never mention the session's error: "10 marks in all", Grade A from 440,
"C minus", "6.5 IELTS" for Grade B, and signing with your own name. From Sessions 4–6: "0900" as
9 pm, "ulcers on her foot" as a rule, "among diabetics patients", "when she discharges", the past
perfect for "the last two months", "semicolons are obsolete", "never use *but*", "*wear* pull-up
pads is wrong", "Thank you for attending" a patient, scores given to single openings or
paragraphs, and grade promises.
