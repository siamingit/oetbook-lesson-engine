Status: **Verified, 2026-10-13** (the chat on oet.com; the owner's rulings recorded in
[ADR 030](adr/030-listening-board-layout.md), decision 6). Every exam fact the
Listening lessons state, with where it is on oet.com. Only the verified facts are
taught, and facts 20 to 22 only as their rulings say (ADR 029, owner decision 9;
ADR 033; AGENTS.md §5).

- **VERIFIED**: the quote was read on the page named, on 2026-10-13, by the agent,
  or confirmed by the chat from https://oet.com/learn/listening the same day.
- **UNVERIFIED**: not found in those words on oet.com; never taught until verified.

## The facts

| # | Fact the lessons state | Source and quote | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listening has three parts and 42 questions | L: "Three parts in the sub-test"; "42 questions to answer in total" | VERIFIED |
| 2 | Each question is worth one mark: Part A 24 marks, Part B 6, Part C 12 (42 in all) | L: "Part A accounts for 24 marks, Part B accounts for 6 marks, and Part C accounts for 12 marks" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13) |
| 3 | The Listening test takes about 40 minutes | L: "Approximately 40 minutes"; T: "Approximately 40 minutes long" | VERIFIED |
| 4 | Listening is the same for every profession, on general healthcare topics | T: "with topics of generic healthcare interest and accessible to all candidates across all professions" | VERIFIED |
| 5 | Each part is heard once only | L: "You will listen to each part of the test once only"; A: "You only hear the recording once" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13) |
| 6 | Part A: two consultations between a health professional and a patient, about 4.5 to 5 minutes each, 12 gaps each, notes completed with a word or a short phrase | A: "Two audio recordings"; "approximately 4.5 - 5 minutes long"; "12 questions per extract"; "a set of healthcare professional's notes with a word or short phrase"; "a health professional is talking to a patient" | VERIFIED |
| 7 | Part A: 30 seconds to look at the notes before each recording | A: "30 seconds to look at the notes before the audio starts playing" | VERIFIED |
| 8 | Part A: write the words you hear, without paraphrasing; marks are lost for repeating words from the notes, adding information not in the recording, contradicting yourself or being unclear | L: "you must complete the notes using the same words you hear on the recording"; "You should not paraphrase the information"; "You will lose marks if you repeat the words from the notes, add information that is not in the recording, or if you contradict yourself or make your meaning unclear" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13) |
| 9 | Part A answers are generally one to three words; do not change the word form or add extra words | A: "generally between one and three words"; must not "change the word form or add extra words" | VERIFIED |
| 10 | Spelling matters in Listening: marks may be lost for spelling mistakes; UK and US spellings are accepted and minor variations may be accepted if the meaning is clear | L: "you may lose marks for spelling mistakes"; A: "UK and US spellings are accepted and minor spelling variations may be accepted, as long as the intended meaning is clear" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13: spelling of common medical terms matters) |
| 11 | Part B: six short workplace recordings (handovers, training sessions, briefings, colleagues talking, a patient and a health professional), one or two speakers, at least one a health professional, about 45 seconds each | B: "Six audio recordings of workplace interactions"; "Approximately 45 seconds each"; "One or two speakers"; "one of them will always be a healthcare professional" | VERIFIED |
| 12 | Part B: one question per recording, with three options (A, B, C) | B: "One 3-option multiple-choice question per audio" | VERIFIED |
| 13 | Part B: 15 seconds to read the question before each recording | B: "15 seconds to prepare" | VERIFIED |
| 14 | Part C: two recordings, presentations or interviews (two of one kind or one of each), about 4 to 5 minutes each, six questions each with three options | C: "Two audio recordings"; "Presentation or interview"; "around 4-5 minutes"; "Six 3-option multiple-choice questions per audio" | VERIFIED |
| 15 | Part C: 90 seconds to read the questions before each recording | C: "Before the audio begins, you have 90 seconds to read through the questions" | VERIFIED |
| 16 | On paper, two minutes at the end to check all answers; on computer and OET@Home, a short pause after each question | L: "If you take OET on paper, you will have two minutes at the end of the Listening sub-test to check your answers"; "If you take OET on computer or OET@Home, there will be a short pause at the end of each question to check your answers" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13) |
| 17 | Part A answers are written in the booklet; Parts B and C answers are shaded | L: "For Part A, you must write your answers in the space provided in the question booklet"; "For Part B and Part C, you must shade the lozenge next to the appropriate answer" | VERIFIED |
| 18 | Grade B (350) typically needs at least 30 of 42 marks; boundaries vary slightly by test version | L: "Test-takers awarded grade B (a scale score of 350) will typically have a score of at least 30 marks"; "The grade boundaries are adjusted slightly for each test" | VERIFIED (chat, 2026-10-13). | 20 | Part A: answers may use common abbreviations | not found on L, A; oet.com stresses writing the words heard (fact 8) | **NOT TAUGHT** (owner, 2026-10-13): teach writing the words heard |
| 21 | Part A: a time to read the general instructions before Part A, usable as extra reading time | not found on L, A | **NOT TAUGHT** (owner, 2026-10-13): teach only the 30 seconds to look at the notes before each extract (fact 7) |
| 22 | A target of about 20 of 24 in Part A (or 4 of 6 in Part B, 8 of 12 in Part C) | no oet.com source; a study target the instructor set | **ONLY AS THE TEACHER'S OWN RECOMMENDATION, in the first person** (owner, 2026-10-14, [ADR 033](adr/033-owner-review-listening-01-02-narration.md); it replaces "the instructor's suggested targets" of 2026-10-13): "I'd recommend aiming for 20 out of 24 in Part A.", with fact 18 said as: "Grade B usually needs about 30 of the 42 Listening marks." |

## Not taught (corrected silently, owner decision 9)

The source sessions also say: four options in Part C (it is three, fact 14), 38
questions (it is 42, fact 1), 30 seconds of reading time in Part B (it is 15, fact
13), and that an echocardiogram is an ECG. The lessons teach the correct version
and never mention the error.
