What is nominalisation?
Nominalisationturning a verb or an adjective into a noun
We analysed the data from the experiment, and it revealed that children react when they have too much sugar.
Nominalisation
analyseverb analysisnoun
reactverb reactionnoun
when they have too much sugarclause excessive sugar intakenoun phrase
✓ The analysis of the data revealed children's reaction to excessive sugar intake.
excessive = too much  ·  intake = what you eat or drink
Nominalisation makes your sentences more formal.
