## House rule: never name an app (owner, 2026-11-12)

A lesson never names Zoom or any other app, and never says the Speaking test is a video call. Lessons say only that the interlocutor plays the patient. A line mentions the mode only when it truly needs it, and then it gives both ways: face to face on paper at a test venue, online on computer and at home (fact 12).

UNKNOWN on the pages read: whether the interlocutor is a healthcare professional; the exact card
layout on the current test; the score conversion formula; any change to Speaking since 2024.

## Course analysis (summary)

Done on 2026-11-08 (agent, owner asleep). Nothing has been built and no decision is final until the
owner reviews it. Full text: `C:\OET\lessons\_speaking-analysis\ANALYSIS.md`.

- **Sources.** Six Persian sessions, 6 h 9 min, 113 slides. They follow the owner's five stages:
  starting, history, explaining, reassuring and educating, ending. Nothing teaches stage 5. No
  learner speaks in any session.
- **Cost.** Scribe v2: 4,567 credits, about $1.66. Gemini: 0 minutes, $0, because the three
  off-slide stretches were clear from the transcripts and frames. Cartesia: $0.
- **His method is the base.** Read the card in a fixed order. Go from open to closed questions,
  with polite indirect frames. Explore a concern before explaining. Explain in threes, then
  check. Ask before advising. Acknowledge before persuading. With an angry patient, never start
  by explaining.
- **Corrected from oet.com.** These come from the sessions and are not repeated in lessons:
  - "ticks" and negative marks: there are two assessors, bands of 0–6 and 0–3, and mistakes
    are not counted;
  - a test centre: corrected by the owner on 2026-11-12 (fact 12). On paper at a test venue the test is face to face; on computer and at home it is online;
  - "5 to 6 minutes";
  - "never ask the name": OET advises checking it;
  - "set phrases raise the score";
  - card frequencies, which OET does not publish.
- **Proposed course: 15 sessions, about 9 hours.**
  - Course order: The Test and the Role-Play; Reading the Card; Starting the Role-Play;
    Sounding Clear; Asking Questions; Taking a History; Keeping Going; Finding the Patient's
    Concerns; Showing Empathy and Reassuring; Explaining Clearly; Giving Advice; Persuading a
    Reluctant Patient; When a Patient Is Angry or Upset; Ending the Role-Play; A Whole
    Role-Play.
  - New (authored): Sounding Clear, Keeping Going, Empathy, Angry or Upset (part adapted),
    Ending, A Whole Role-Play.
  - Providing structure is taught where it is used, not as a session. Full card walk-throughs
    go to the profession courses.
- **Speaking is the backbone.** Each lesson has "your turn" gaps, using the existing pause
  cue, and speaking practice points. There are 75 points, 54 of them `speak_repeat`,
  `speak_respond` or `roleplay`. Each item carries the data the site's speaking types will
  need.
- **Voices.** The patient is Gemma (female) or Archie (male), both on `sonic-3.6` at speed
  0.8, chosen by the owner by ear on 2026-11-09 (ADR 027 amendment of 2026-11-09). Rupert
  stays the teacher. The engine needs a `speaker` field per narration line, a shared-code
  change to be proved on every built lesson.
- **Handouts.** The sessions teach and practise the moves. H28 (questions) and H29 (lay terms,
  phrasal verbs) hold the full lists and are built after the sessions are scripted.
- **Nine open questions** for the owner, each with a recommendation, are in §9 of the analysis.
